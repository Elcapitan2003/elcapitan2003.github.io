About

A tutor who got curious about how things work under the hood.

I'm based in Rosenheim, Germany, currently studying Applied Artificial Intelligence at TH Rosenheim. Before that, I spent two years tutoring Mathematics, Physics, and Computer Science online to students preparing for Cambridge examinations.

Teaching taught me to break big ideas into small, honest steps, and to never accept "it just works" as an answer. That same instinct—ask how, ask why, then build—is what I bring to learning software and AI engineering now.

Teaching

Why I keep coming back to it

Two years of tutoring, and what it actually taught me about how to learn.

I started tutoring right after my own A-Levels — a friend needed help with calculus, and one session turned into two years of teaching Mathematics, Physics, and Computer Science internationally.

What kept me in it wasn't just the subject matter, but the moment a concept finally clicks for someone. I learned to explain the same idea five different ways until one lands. That instinct for finding the right mental model is what I bring to writing code today.
