About

From the whiteboard to the terminal.

I've spent the last two years tutoring math online, picking it up right after finishing my A-levels. What started as helping a few students became something I actually built a reputation on — students keep coming back, and a lot of them tell their friends.

Teaching taught me something I didn't expect: the best way to understand a hard idea is to break it down until someone else can rebuild it themselves. That's the same instinct I'm now pointing at software engineering.

This site is where both live — the notes I've written for students over two years, and the projects, mistakes, and small wins from teaching myself to build software. Consider it a public notebook.
