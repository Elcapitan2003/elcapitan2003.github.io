about

A tutor who got curious about how things work under the hood.

I'm based in Rosenheim, Germany, currently studying Applied Artificial Intelligence. Before that, I spent two years tutoring online — Mathematics, Physics, and Computer Science — to students preparing for their Cambridge exams.

Teaching taught me to break big ideas into small, honest steps, and to never accept "it just works" as an answer. That same instinct — ask how, ask why, then build — is what I bring to learning software and AI now.

This site is where I'm documenting that shift: the projects I'm building, the notes I've written for students, and everything in between.

teaching is a passion

Why I keep coming back to it

Two years of tutoring, and what it actually taught me.

I started tutoring right after my own A Levels, almost by accident — a friend needed help with calculus, and one session turned into two years of teaching Mathematics, Physics, and Computer Science to students across Bangladesh and internationally.

What kept me in it wasn't the subject matter — it was the moment a concept finally clicks for someone. I've learned to explain the same idea five different ways until one of them lands, and I think that instinct for finding the right explanation is what I bring to everything I do now, including learning to code.

I also coached students through IELTS preparation, and more recently started experimenting with GenAI tools to build sharper, more personalized teaching materials — an early sign of where my interests were heading.

  • Tutored 7 students (2 local, 5 international) for O and A Level exams, averaging 15 hours/week.
